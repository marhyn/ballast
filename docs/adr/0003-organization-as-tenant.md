# Organization (via better-auth's organization plugin) is the tenant, and users may belong to several

A User can belong to multiple Organizations and acts as one at a time, tracked via `session.activeOrganizationId`; better-auth's `organization` plugin supplies the Organization/Member/Invitation tables directly rather than us building tenancy by hand. We picked the multi-org shape over strict single-tenant-per-user, even though most Ballast-derived apps may start single-tenant, because it's a strict superset: a single-tenant product simply never renders "invite member" / "switch org" UI, whereas the reverse — bolting multi-tenancy onto a single-tenant schema later — is a real migration. "Organization" is the one canonical term for this concept; "Tenant" and "Workspace" are explicitly avoided (see `CONTEXT.md`).
